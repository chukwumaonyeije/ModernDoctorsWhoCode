## Known link debt

The initial scan found 25 existing internal-link failures. They are recorded in the route baseline so Stage 0 can distinguish existing debt from new regressions.

The failures fall into four groups:

1. Relative article links that resolve beneath the current article URL instead of `/blog/`.
2. Five tag links emitted by the internal MFM scoring page for tag routes that are not generated by the current article taxonomy.
3. Placeholder or malformed destinations such as `link-to-upload` and an email address placed in an `href` without `mailto:`.
4. Older route forms such as `/posts/...` or a renamed article slug.

The complete source-to-target list remains in `baseline/protected-routes.json`. Stage 0 does not rewrite those articles. A later maintenance pass can repair the known list and then regenerate the baseline with zero known failures.

## Guardrail behavior

The validator fails when:

- `dist/` does not exist.
- A protected HTML page disappears.
- A protected critical endpoint disappears.
- A new generated internal link points to a missing local target.

It permits:

- New pages.
- New endpoints outside the protected list.
- The 25 explicitly recorded baseline issues.

The link scanner ignores external destinations, fragment-only links, email and telephone links, data URLs, JavaScript URLs, and anchor-like strings inside scripts and styles.

## Curriculum validation boundary

Duplicate lesson order and missing course, path, project, or article references cannot be validated until the educational collections exist. Stage 1 will make those relationships typed in Astro and add the cross-collection checks as part of the first curriculum slice.
