A plane passes through a box. The learner must connect that plane with the section it produces. Each lesson develops the same teaching package through Meaning → Representation → Inspection.

| Lesson | Task | Practice minutes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Define What the Learner Must See | 30 |
| 2 | Build an SVG You Can Correct | 50 |
| 3 | Make the Diagram Readable | 40 |
| 4 | Add Motion with HyperFrames | 65 |
| 5 | Use 3D to Answer a Spatial Question | 70 |
| 6 | Deliver a Visual You Can Maintain | 45 |

The planned practice totals 300 minutes, excluding installation, troubleshooting, and reviewer availability. Course duration is derived from the six published lessons.

The capstone contains editable sources, exports, text alternatives, provenance, and a demonstrated correction. The proposed completion standard is 10/12 with no zero scores and no unresolved material geometry, orientation, or content errors. This curriculum and rubric have not been piloted.

The optional anatomical extension requires an educator-supplied reference and appropriate content review. It is not required for completion.
