The Middle Path teaches physicians to assess AI claims and decide what follows for a specific workflow. Every part develops the same written assessment: what the evidence supports, what controls surround the model, and who can intervene when it fails.

Part I includes a clinical case, five questions with answer explanations, and a reusable review worksheet. Parts II through IV are planned: recursive self-improvement evidence; evaluations, pacing, and governance; and the clinical harness in practice. The duration currently covers Part I only and will grow as lessons are added.

Part I includes article narration and a separate companion podcast alongside the written lesson.
