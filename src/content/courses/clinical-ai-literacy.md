The model generates. The prompt specifies. The agent coordinates. The harness governs. The physician remains accountable.

This course moves from individual instructions to system-level supervision. It treats Claude Code, Google Antigravity, OpenAI Codex, and DeepSeek Harness as dated architectural examples rather than clinical products, then applies their visible controls to a bounded clinical workflow.
