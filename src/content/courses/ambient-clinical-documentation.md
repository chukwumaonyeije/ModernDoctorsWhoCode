Ambient documentation is becoming a baseline feature of clinical software rather than a novelty. This course treats it as a workflow to design, not a product to adopt: a physician's own history from paper charts to human dictation to ambient AI, a repeatable three-phase workflow, a disciplined review habit, and a ten-point framework for evaluating any platform without becoming attached to one.
