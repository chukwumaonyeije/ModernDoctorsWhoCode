Choose one recurring clinical or clinical-adjacent workflow. Define its evidence boundary, permitted tools, verification rules, physician checkpoint, execution limit, and audit record before granting the system any consequential authority.

The finished project should include a SCOPE specification, Harness Card, evidence map, deterministic verification plan, three-error check, physician checkpoint, and five synthetic or properly de-identified test cases.
